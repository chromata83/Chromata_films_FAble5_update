— On your phone

Save a film to your phone

iPhone and iPad

  1. Tap Download next to the film you want.
  2. Safari asks whether you want to download the file. Tap Download.
  3. Tap the ↓ icon at the top right of Safari, then tap your film.
  4. Tap the Share button, then Save Video. The film is now in your Photos app. It is also kept in the Files app, under Downloads.

Android

  1. Tap Download next to the film you want, and confirm if Chrome asks.
  2. When the download is complete, tap the notification, or open Files and go to Downloads.
  3. Your gallery app (Google Photos for example) will show the film alongside your other videos.

Tips: use Wi-Fi, because the files are large. If you opened this page from WhatsApp, Instagram or an email app and nothing downloads, open the link in Safari or Chrome instead.

— On your TV

Watch on the big screen

Option 1 · Send it from your phone

  1. Save the film to your phone first (see "On your phone").
  2. Apple TV or an AirPlay TV: open the film in Photos, tap Share, then AirPlay, and choose your TV.
  3. Android TV, Google TV or Chromecast: open the film in Google Photos and tap the Cast icon.

Option 2 · A USB stick

  1. Download the film on a computer and copy it to a USB stick. Format the stick as exFAT, because the older FAT32 format cannot hold files larger than 4 GB.
  2. Plug the stick into your TV or TV box, open its media player or USB source, and choose the film.

Your phone and your TV box must be on the same Wi-Fi network for AirPlay and Cast. You can also use the AirPlay or Cast icon of the player above to send a film straight to your TV.
