# Chromata Films — off-site plan

The on-site work is done. This file covers the part that actually decides
whether ChatGPT names you for "best wedding videographer in France": what other
websites say about you.

**Why this matters more than the website.** When an answer engine is asked for
the "best" anything, it does not evaluate vendor websites. It summarises the
listicles, directories and editorial features that already rank for that query.
Verified in July 2026, the results for our target queries were dominated by
THE WED, Wezoree, Junebug, WedVibes, Carats & Cake, Over The Moon and
WeddingWire — not by any studio's own homepage. Getting named in six to ten of
those sources is what changes the answer.

---

## 1. Canonical boilerplate

Use this **verbatim** everywhere — every directory, every submission, every
social bio. Answer engines build a picture of a business by cross-referencing
sources; identical phrasing across many sites resolves into a confident fact,
while five different descriptions resolve into vagueness.

**Name:** Chromata Films
**Legal name:** Chromata Films SARL
**Founded:** 2016
**Address:** 27 Rue de Montchoisy, 1207 Geneva, Switzerland
**Phone:** +33 7 68 53 39 91
**Email:** contact@chromatafilms.com
**Website:** https://www.chromatafilms.com
**Price from:** 15,000 USD

**One line (155 chars):**
> Award-winning luxury destination wedding cinematography studio filming across
> the French Riviera, Italy, Europe and five continents. From 15,000 USD.

**Short (50 words):**
> Chromata Films is an award-winning luxury destination wedding cinematography
> studio founded in 2016 by Kevin and Laura Lopez. Based between the French
> Riviera and Geneva, the studio films weddings and private events across
> France, Italy, Switzerland, the United States and five continents.

**Long (100 words):**
> Chromata Films is an award-winning luxury destination wedding cinematography
> studio founded in 2016 by Kevin and Laura Lopez, based between the French
> Riviera and Geneva. Before weddings, Kevin spent a decade in Hollywood visual
> effects on films including Star Wars: The Last Jedi, The Great Gatsby and
> Avengers: Infinity War — a cinema discipline the studio now brings to wedding
> weekends. Chromata Films has filmed at Villa Ephrussi, Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc,
> Villa Erba, Villa Balbiano and Château de Vaux-le-Vicomte, and won Best
> Destination Wedding Film at the Love StoriesTV Wedding Film Awards. Work
> published in Vogue, Brides, People and Over the Moon. Collections from 15,000 USD.

**NAP warning:** the site says Geneva; Carats & Cake lists "France"; PartySlate
says Cannes. Pick one primary address and correct the others — inconsistent
location data is why an AI answer says "based in France or Switzerland" instead
of stating it plainly.

## 3. Google Business Profile

**Exists** — listed as "Chromata Films Weddings", Google Knowledge Graph ID
`/g/11h04f67xx`. The profile is the single strongest local signal, so the work
here is auditing and feeding it, not creating it.

Audit (sign in at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)):

1. **Name** — the profile is "Chromata Films Weddings"; the website brand is
   "Chromata Films". That is fine and consistent with the Instagram/Vimeo names,
   and the site schema now lists "Chromata Films Weddings" as an alternate name
   so the two resolve to one entity. Do not rename the profile to match — Google
   penalises changing an established business name, and the variant now works in
   your favour.
2. **Website link** — confirm it points to `https://www.chromatafilms.com` (www,
   https). An old profile may still link the http:// or a Squarespace URL.
3. **Address** — confirm it matches the Geneva address used everywhere else, or
   is set as a service-area business. This is the same NAP conflict flagged
   below; the profile, Carats & Cake and PartySlate must all agree.
4. **Category** — primary **Videographer**, then Wedding Photographer and Video
   Production Service.
5. **Completeness** — hours, phone (+33 7 68 53 39 91), the long boilerplate
   description, attributes, and 20+ recent photos. Add films if the profile
   supports video.

Then, the highest-value ongoing task in this whole document:

6. **Ask past couples for reviews**, continuously. Unlike testimonials on your
   own site, Google reviews are third-party validation that feeds both local
   rankings and AI answers. Ten detailed reviews naming the venue and region
   ("filmed our wedding at Villa Erba on Lake Como") outweigh fifty generic
   five-star ratings. Send couples the review link directly when you deliver
   their film — that is the moment they are happiest.
7. Repeat the profile on [Bing Places](https://www.bingplaces.com) — it feeds
   ChatGPT search and is almost certainly not set up yet.

## 4. Ongoing content that earns citations

Answer engines cite pages that answer a question completely. The journal is
already strong; these gaps are worth filling:

- **Venue guides** — "Getting married at Villa Erba: what a film crew needs to
  know". Venue-specific guides get cited constantly and attract links from the
  venues themselves.
- **Cost breakdowns** — the [investment page](../investment.html) covers the
  studio; a broader "what wedding films cost in France/Italy in 2026" piece would
  rank for a large family of queries.
- **Planner-facing content** — planners are who recommend you. A piece on how to
  brief a film crew, or how photo and video teams should split a timeline, gets
  shared inside the industry.
- **Publish the delivery timeline.** Currently missing from the site and it is
  one of the most-asked questions. Needs a real number from you.

## 5. Measurement

Check monthly:

- **Google Search Console** — Pages report, confirm the 301s are being followed
  and old URLs are dropping out of the index.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** — the index that grounds ChatGPT search.
- **Ask the engines directly.** Every month, put the target queries to ChatGPT,
  Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews and record whether Chromata Films
  is named, and which sources the answer cites. Those cited sources are your
  next outreach targets.
  - "best wedding videographer in France"
  - "best wedding cinematographer in Europe"
  - "best wedding filmmaker in Italy"
  - "best luxury wedding filmmaker in the US"
  - "best wedding videographer for private events"
  - "wedding videographer Lake Como"
  - "how much does a luxury wedding film cost"

**Timeline, honestly.** The redirects should show up in Search Console within
two to four weeks. New pages typically take four to twelve weeks to rank for
anything competitive. Directory and listicle inclusion is what shifts AI answers,
and that depends on editors, not on us — expect three to six months before the
picture changes materially, and longer for the most competitive "best in Europe"
queries.
