# Outreach — ready to send

Fifteen drafts, in the priority order from [seo-outreach-plan.md](seo-outreach-plan.md).

**Before you send anything**, check the target's current list. If they have
already updated it, name the new piece and the new date — nothing kills a pitch
faster than referencing an article from two years ago as though it's current.

**Rules that make these work:**
- Subject lines are specific, never "Collaboration opportunity".
- No adjectives about yourselves in the first paragraph. Editors discount
  "award-winning" instantly; they do not discount "filmed at Villa Balbiano".
- One concrete gap in *their* piece, not a list of your virtues.
- Two links maximum. A full film beats a showreel — it proves you can hold a
  story, which is what separates you from the reel-only studios.
- Send from contact@chromatafilms.com, signed by Kevin. A named director gets
  replies; a studio address gets filed.
- Follow up **once**, after 10–14 days. Then stop.

**Assets to have ready before you start:** two full films (recommend the Lake
Como award-winner and Jacqueline & Gordon), 10–15 high-res stills with vendor
credits, the 50-word and 100-word boilerplate, and the two press links.

## 10–13. Directory profiles — Zankyou, Mariages.net, Matrimonio.com, The Knot

No email needed; these are self-service. For each:

1. Create the profile with the **canonical boilerplate**, unchanged.
2. Set the service area to the countries you actually film in — do not select
   every region, it dilutes you.
3. Price from 15,000 USD.
4. Upload 15+ images and at least one full film.
5. Link `https://www.chromatafilms.com`.
6. **Ask three past couples to review you on each** as soon as the profile is
   live. An empty profile ranks nowhere.

Mariages.net and Matrimonio.com matter disproportionately — they dominate French
and Italian search results, and almost no international studio bothers with them.

**Google Business Profile note:** the profile already exists as "Chromata Films
Weddings" (Knowledge Graph ID `/g/11h04f67xx`). Do not create a second one —
duplicates get both suspended. The action there is auditing and, above all,
collecting reviews; see section 3 of the plan doc.
